## Hi, I'm Erkan

I'm a software developer at [Koç University](https://www.ku.edu.tr) in İstanbul, Türkiye.
I build and maintain internal platforms — .NET backends, microservices, and the frontends that sit on top of them.

This site is where I write down the things I had to figure out the hard way: architecture decisions,
debugging sessions that took longer than they should have, and small tricks worth remembering.

Posts here are written in **English** or **Turkish**. Each post is tagged with its language, so you can
filter by [`en`](/tags/en/) or [`tr`](/tags/tr/).

### Where to find me

- GitHub — [@eguzelkucuk](https://github.com/eguzelkucuk)
